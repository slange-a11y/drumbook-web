What this site is

This page introduces Drumbook, a privately developed practice journal for the drum kit. It is a private project. Nothing is sold here and no contracts are concluded. The app itself is not publicly available at present.

Statements about the state of development describe the situation at the time this page was last changed. They are not a promise of future availability, features or prices.

The short version on privacy

This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from third-party servers — no fonts, no maps, no videos, no social buttons. Everything you see is served from the same address as the page itself. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Server logs

When you open the page your browser necessarily transmits data to GitHub's servers: your IP address, the date and time, the address requested and the amount of data transferred, your browser and operating system identifier, and possibly the page you came from.

The extent and retention of these logs are outside my control and I do not access them. Processing may involve a transfer to the USA, based on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and, additionally, standard contractual clauses. See GitHub's privacy statement and its notes on GitHub Pages.

Getting in touch by email

There is no form on this site. The "Write an email" button merely opens your own email program with some text prepared — nothing is transmitted at that point. Only when you send the message yourself do I receive what it contains: your sender address, your name if you give one, and the text.

Purpose: answering your message and, if you ask for it, a single notification once Drumbook is ready to try.
Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (the consent you give by sending the message) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for replying to enquiries.
Retention: your address is kept only for that one notification and deleted afterwards, and at the latest when the project ends. It is not passed to anyone else and is not used for a newsletter or for advertising.
Withdrawal: you can ask by email at any time, without formality, to have your address deleted.

Email is sent and received through the respective providers. An unencrypted email can be read in transit.

The app itself

This notice concerns the website. As for the app, which is not publicly available yet: Drumbook stores everything solely on the device it is installed on. It has no user accounts, no server connection, no analytics and no advertising identifier. No data is transmitted to me or to anyone else. A separate privacy notice for the app will follow when it is released.
