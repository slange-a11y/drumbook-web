Join the test

Practise with a plan, not a hunch.

Drumbook puts today's practice list together, holds the tempo and remembers what your teacher said. So that at the end of the week there is not just a feeling but a record. It is being tested right now: leave your address and the invitation comes by email.

Ask for an invitation

You get an invitation to TestFlight, Apple's testing app, free of charge. That is the only email you'll get from me, and then I delete your address. TestFlight tells you about new test versions itself.

Write an email

Opens your mail app with the text ready. If that does not work, write to silvio@drumbook.de.

Your address is only used to send you that one message, and deleted afterwards.

  • You open the app and know what is on. Tell Drumbook how many minutes you have. The practice list puts itself together and pulls in what has been left too long.
  • The click keeps running when both hands hold sticks. Even with the screen locked, and it does not drift, because every beat is worked into the sound in advance.
  • What your teacher said is there while you practise. Exercises you were set go into your library and get priority. And your teacher gets a PDF report instead of a shrug.
  • You talk straight to the person building it. Feedback goes out from the test app. I read every word, and often it is in the app a week later.

So you know what you are getting into

Drumbook will later be a subscription, probably three to five euros a month. While it is being tested it costs nothing, and everyone still gets a trial period later on. There is no account and no server of mine: everything stays on your device, and syncing between iPhone and iPad runs through your own iCloud. It is built by one person who is learning the drums.
